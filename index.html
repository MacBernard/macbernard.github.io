Flagship product

MacHub. A complete school, in one system.

MacHub is our school management system, built from real Nigerian school operations, not a foreign template. It runs a school's daily work from one place and speaks the language of terms, sessions, arms and report cards.

  • Student registration and profiles, from admission to graduation
  • Staff management with role-based access: what a cashier sees is not what a class teacher sees
  • Fees: payments, balances and classroom-level reporting
  • Attendance, continuous assessment and exam scoring
  • Report sheets and cumulative results, generated not hand-written
  • Sessions and terms handled the way Nigerian schools actually run them

Running on a school's own computer or server, MacHub works without depending on the internet, and each school carries its own name, identity and settings.

Flagship product

MacSuite. The whole hotel, from one desk.

MacSuite is our hotel management system, built for the way Nigerian hotels, lodges and guest houses actually operate. Every booking, room, bill and bar order lives in one system, with nothing balanced by hand.

  • Reservations from walk-in, phone or online requests, with live room availability
  • Fast check-in and check-out, with the full bill generated automatically
  • Room types, seasonal rates and maintenance scheduling
  • Guest profiles and stay history, so returning guests are treated like it
  • Housekeeping status tracked room by room
  • Restaurant and bar POS charges posted straight to the guest bill

MacSuite runs on a modular subscription: start with the accommodation base, then add restaurant POS, bar POS, laundry, inventory or accounting only when you need them. Deployed on the hotel's own computers or in the cloud.
